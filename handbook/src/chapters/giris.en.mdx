## One idea: turn the image into words

Language models can only read sequences of tokens. PaliGemma's trick is simple: it turns the image into a sequence of tokens too. SigLIP cuts the photo into 256 pieces and produces one vector per piece. The bridge brings these vectors to the same size as Gemma's word vectors. To Gemma there are now 256 "visual words" followed by a few real words. Gemma reads them and writes the answer word by word, as usual.
